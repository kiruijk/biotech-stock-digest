Company Overview

Kailera Therapeutics (KLRA) is a Waltham, Massachusetts-based clinical-stage biotech focused on obesity and related metabolic conditions. Founded in 2024, the company holds exclusive global rights outside Greater China to four metabolic assets licensed from Jiangsu Hengrui Pharmaceuticals, and listed on Nasdaq in April 2026.

The lead program, ribupatide injection (KAI-9531), is a once-weekly GLP-1/GIP receptor dual agonist — the same mechanism as Eli Lilly's Mounjaro/Zepbound — now in the global Phase 3 KaiNETIC program enrolling 3,700+ participants. A Phase 2b high-dose trial is fully enrolled, and an oral formulation (ribupatide oral, KAI-9531-T) has an active IND with Phase 3 planned for 1H 2027.

Behind ribupatide sit KAI-7535, an oral small-molecule GLP-1 receptor agonist in a global Phase 2 obesity trial, and KAI-4729, an injectable GLP-1/GIP/glucagon tri-agonist expected to enter Phase 1 in 2026. The mix of injectable peptides, oral peptides and oral small molecules gives Kailera multiple shots on goal across formats.

Investor thesis: KLRA is a well-funded late-stage obesity play with a pipeline that mirrors the Lilly playbook (dual agonist → oral → tri-agonist). Hengrui's parallel trials in China provide an additional data stream. The key debate is differentiation: ribupatide must show weight loss and tolerability competitive with tirzepatide to win share, and pivotal Phase 3 data isn't expected until 2028. The stock trades well below its April 2026 IPO-era levels after reports of GI side effects.
