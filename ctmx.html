Company Overview

CytomX Therapeutics (CTMX) is a South San Francisco-based company whose PROBODY platform masks antibodies and drug conjugates so they activate mainly in tumors, sparing healthy tissue.

Lead program varsetatug masetecan (Varseta-M, CX-2051) is an EpCAM-directed antibody-drug conjugate. EpCAM is a well-known tumor target that has been hard to drug because of toxicity in normal tissue. Phase 1 monotherapy enrollment in colorectal cancer is complete (113 patients), with a data update expected by end of 2026 and a registrational study planned for 1H 2027. CX-801, a masked interferon, is in Phase 1 in melanoma.

Investor thesis: Varseta-M could open a new target class in late-line colorectal cancer and other GI tumors. Partner revenue (including $37M from Regeneron in July 2026) supports a runway into 2H 2028.
