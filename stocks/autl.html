Autolus Therapeutics (AUTL)

Cell Therapy

Company Overview

Autolus Therapeutics (AUTL) is a London-based commercial-stage CAR-T company. Its product AUCATZYL (obecabtagene autoleucel, obe-cel) is approved for adults with relapsed/refractory B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL).

AUCATZYL uses a fast off-rate CD19 binder designed to reduce the severe immune toxicities seen with other CAR-Ts. Q2 2026 net product revenue was $45.7M (+119% year over year), and full-year guidance was raised to $140–150M. Obe-cel is also in trials for lupus nephritis, pediatric ALL and progressive multiple sclerosis.

Investor thesis: AUTL is a launch-execution story with autoimmune optionality. Revenue is growing quickly and gross margins are improving, but the company still burns cash; a $250M Perceptive credit facility ($75M drawn) adds flexibility.
