Allogene Therapeutics (ALLO)

Cell Therapy

Company Overview

Allogene Therapeutics (ALLO) is a South San Francisco-based developer of allogeneic ("off-the-shelf") CAR-T cell therapies made from healthy donor cells, avoiding the weeks-long manufacturing of patient-specific CAR-T.

Lead program cemacabtagene ansegedleucel (cema-cel) is in the pivotal ALPHA3 trial as first-line consolidation for large B-cell lymphoma patients who still have minimal residual disease (MRD) after chemotherapy. At the interim futility analysis, 58.3% of cema-cel patients became MRD-negative versus 16.7% on observation, with no CRS or ICANS. FDA granted RMAT and Fast Track designations. ALLO-329 targets autoimmune diseases, with data expected in Q4 2026.

Investor thesis: ALLO is a bet that off-the-shelf CAR-T can move into earlier-line treatment where autologous therapy is impractical. With $423.6M in cash and runway into 2029, it can reach the mid-2027 EFS update.
