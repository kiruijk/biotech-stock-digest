Company Overview

UroGen Pharma (URGN) is a Princeton, New Jersey-based commercial-stage company in uro-oncology. Its RTGel hydrogel technology keeps chemotherapy in contact with urinary tract tissue for longer, allowing non-surgical treatment of tumors.

UroGen markets Jelmyto for upper tract urothelial cancer and ZUSDURI (mitomycin intravesical solution) for recurrent low-grade intermediate-risk NMIBC. ZUSDURI sales reached $50.4M in Q2 2026 (+73% vs. Q1), lifting total revenue to $72.5M, and the company is roughly at operating break-even. UGN-103, a next-generation formulation, is on track for an NDA in Q3 2026.

Investor thesis: URGN is a commercial growth story: ZUSDURI offers a non-surgical option for patients who otherwise face repeated surgeries (TURBT). Execution on the launch and UGN-103 lifecycle management are key.
