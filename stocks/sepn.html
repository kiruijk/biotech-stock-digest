Septerna (SEPN)

Obesity & Metabolic

Company Overview

Septerna (SEPN) is a South San Francisco-based biotech whose Native Complex platform recreates GPCRs in their natural state to discover oral small-molecule drugs for targets previously reachable only by injectable peptides.

The lead wholly owned program, SEP-479, is an oral small-molecule PTH1R agonist for hypoparathyroidism. Early pharmacokinetics support once-daily dosing (~3–4 day half-life), with SAD/MAD data expected in Q1 2027. Septerna also has a Novo Nordisk collaboration to discover oral small molecules for obesity, type 2 diabetes and cardiometabolic disease, which generated $26.7M of revenue in Q2 2026.

Investor thesis: SEPN is a platform bet on turning peptide hormones into pills. The Novo deal validates the approach and provides non-dilutive funding, while $516.5M in cash funds operations at least into 2029.
