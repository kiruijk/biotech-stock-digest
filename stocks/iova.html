Company Overview

Iovance Biotherapeutics (IOVA) is a San Carlos, California-based commercial-stage company that makes tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) therapies. Each dose is manufactured from immune cells taken from the patient's own tumor.

Amtagvi (lifileucel) received FDA accelerated approval in February 2024 as the first T cell therapy for a solid tumor, for advanced melanoma after anti-PD-1 treatment. Iovance also sells Proleukin (IL-2), which is given with Amtagvi. Q2 2026 revenue hit a record $99.3M (+66% year over year; ~$91M from Amtagvi) with a 56% gross margin, and the company is reviewing its $350–370M full-year guidance. More than 95 U.S. treatment centers are active, with ~110 expected by year-end. Amtagvi is approved in Australia, and UK and Swiss decisions are expected in 2026–2027.

The pipeline extends TIL beyond melanoma: IOV-LUN-202 in non-small cell lung cancer (sBLA planned for 2027), the Phase 3 TILVANCE-301 in first-line melanoma, the registrational SARATOGA trial in soft tissue sarcomas (FDA Fast Track) and next-generation IOV-4001 and IOV-5001.

Investor thesis: IOVA has turned from a turnaround story into a commercial growth story; shares are up several-fold in 2026 as Amtagvi demand accelerated. Upside comes from lung cancer and international expansion. The main risks are execution under an interim CEO and competition from newer off-the-shelf melanoma therapies.
