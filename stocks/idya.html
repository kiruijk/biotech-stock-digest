Company Overview

IDEAYA Biosciences (IDYA) is a South San Francisco-based precision oncology company focused on synthetic lethality and targeted therapies.

Lead drug darovasertib, a PKC inhibitor combined with crizotinib, met its primary endpoint in the registrational OptimUM-02 trial in metastatic uveal melanoma, cutting the risk of progression by 58% in HLA-A2-negative patients. IDEAYA is submitting an NDA under the FDA's Real-Time Oncology Review for potential accelerated approval, with completion expected in 2H 2026. The pipeline also includes a DLL3-targeting ADC in a registrational trial and MTAP/KRAS combination studies.

Investor thesis: IDYA is transitioning to commercial stage in uveal melanoma, where darovasertib complements KIMMTRAK (which only serves HLA-A2-positive patients). A June 2026 raise brought cash to ~$1.24B.
