Company Overview

Viking Therapeutics (VKTX) is a San Diego-based clinical-stage company focused on metabolic and endocrine diseases, led by co-founder and CEO Brian Lian.

Its lead program, VK2735, is a dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist, the same mechanism as Eli Lilly's Mounjaro/Zepbound, developed in both injectable and oral forms. The subcutaneous Phase 3 VANQUISH program is fully enrolled: VANQUISH-1 (~4,500 adults with obesity) and VANQUISH-2 (~1,000 adults with obesity and type 2 diabetes), each dosing weekly for 78 weeks. The oral tablet produced up to 12.2% weight loss after 13 weeks in the Phase 2 VENTURE-Oral study, and Viking expects to start oral Phase 3 in Q4 2026. Data from a maintenance-dosing study (weekly, every-other-week and monthly regimens) is expected in Q3 2026.

Earlier programs include VK3019, an amylin agonist that entered Phase 1 in Q2 2026, and VK2809, a thyroid hormone receptor-beta agonist studied in MASH.

Investor thesis: VKTX is one of the few independent companies with both an injectable and an oral GLP-1/GIP program in late-stage development, which keeps it at the center of obesity M&A speculation — shares jumped in September 2026 after CNBC flagged Viking as a likely buyout target. The risks are spending and scale: Phase 3 costs pushed the first-half 2026 net loss to $286M, and competing with Lilly and Novo alone would require major manufacturing and commercial investment.
