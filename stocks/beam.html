Beam Therapeutics (BEAM)

Gene Editing & RNA

Company Overview

Beam Therapeutics (BEAM) is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based pioneer of base editing, a precision gene editing technology that changes single DNA letters without cutting both strands of DNA.

BEAM-302, an in vivo base editor for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (AATD), dosed its first patient in a global pivotal cohort; updated data were chosen for a late-breaking oral presentation at ERS 2026. Risto-cel (BEAM-101), an ex vivo therapy for sickle cell disease, has completed dosing in its Phase 1/2 BEACON trial, with a BLA possible as early as year-end 2026.

Investor thesis: BEAM has two late-stage shots: an in vivo AATD therapy that could be a one-time cure, and a potentially best-in-class SCD product. $1.2B in cash plus a Sixth Street facility funds operations into mid-2029.
