Compass Therapeutics (CMPX)

Targeted Oncology

Company Overview

Compass Therapeutics (CMPX) is a Boston-based oncology company developing bispecific antibodies.

Lead program tovecimig (CTX-009) is a DLL4 x VEGF-A bispecific. In the Phase 2/3 COMPANION-002 trial in previously treated biliary tract cancer (BTC), tovecimig plus paclitaxel achieved an 18.0% response rate versus 5.3% for paclitaxel alone and improved median progression-free survival (4.7 vs. 2.6 months), but overall survival did not reach statistical significance, which Compass attributes to study design and patient crossover. On September 22, 2026, the FDA recommended an additional trial showing a survival benefit before a BLA submission. Compass disagrees and says it will keep preparing a BLA based on COMPANION-002. Earlier programs include CTX-8371 (PD-1 x PD-L1) and CTX-10726 (PD-1 x VEGF-A).

Investor thesis: CMPX is now a high-risk regulatory standoff. Shares fell nearly 40% on the FDA news. Filing without FDA support risks a refusal or rejection, while a new survival trial would take years and likely require significant new capital. The ESMO data and any follow-up FDA meetings are the near-term swing factors.
