Company Overview

Caribou Biosciences (CRBU) is a Berkeley, California-based company co-founded by CEO Rachel Haurwitz and Nobel laureate Jennifer Doudna. It uses its chRDNA gene editing technology to build allogeneic (off-the-shelf) CAR-T cell therapies.

Lead program vispa-cel (CB-010) is an anti-CD19 allogeneic CAR-T with a PD-1 knockout. Long-term ANTLER data in second-line large B cell lymphoma showed responses on par with autologous CAR-T. A pivotal Phase 3 (ANTLER-3) is planned for the ~75% of second-line patients who don't receive autologous CAR-T, but Caribou is still seeking funding for it. CB-011 (anti-BCMA) is in dose expansion for multiple myeloma.

Investor thesis: CRBU trades near its cash value. A partnership to fund ANTLER-3 would be the key catalyst; without one, the pivotal program may stall.
