Company Overview

enGene Therapeutics (ENGN) is a Montreal-based company developing non-viral gene therapies delivered directly into the bladder.

Lead program detalimogene voraplasmid delivers the immune-stimulating IL-12 gene into bladder tissue for BCG-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC). In May 2026, interim Phase 2 LEGEND data showed a 54% complete response rate at any time but weak durability (25% at 12 months by Kaplan-Meier estimate), and the stock fell over 80%. enGene still plans to begin its BLA submission in Q4 2026 and is testing a surfactant-enhanced version to improve durability.

Investor thesis: ENGN is now a deep-value, high-risk situation. Its ~$0.1B market cap is well below its $266.3M cash, so investors are assigning negative value to the pipeline. Any evidence of better durability could drive a sharp recovery.
