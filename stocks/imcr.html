Immunocore (IMCR)

MelanomaTargeted Oncology

Company Overview

Immunocore Holdings (IMCR) is a commercial-stage biotech headquartered in Abingdon, UK, that pioneered ImmTAX — soluble T cell receptor bispecifics that redirect the immune system to target cancer, infectious disease and autoimmune disease. Founded in 1999, it trades on Nasdaq.

Its first product, KIMMTRAK (tebentafusp), is approved for unresectable or metastatic uveal melanoma and has shown a doubling of five-year survival. KIMMTRAK generated $115.9M in Q2 2026 net sales (up 18% year over year), bringing Immunocore close to break-even — a rarity among the stocks tracked here.

The pipeline centers on melanoma expansion: TEBE-AM (tebentafusp in previously treated cutaneous melanoma, Phase 3, topline data by end of 2026), PRISM-MEL-301 (brenetafusp + nivolumab in first-line cutaneous melanoma, Phase 3) and ATOM (adjuvant uveal melanoma, Phase 3). Earlier programs target PRAME-positive tumors, HIV, type 1 diabetes and dermatology.

Investor thesis: IMCR offers a de-risked, revenue-generating base with Phase 3 upside. Success in cutaneous melanoma — a much larger market than uveal melanoma — would transform the growth outlook. The main risks are TEBE-AM execution and whether the platform extends beyond melanoma.

Risk Factors

TEBE-AM Readout
A negative result in cutaneous melanoma would cap KIMMTRAK's growth to the small uveal melanoma market.
HIGH RISK
Single-Product Revenue
All revenue comes from KIMMTRAK; any pricing or competitive pressure directly affects the business.
MEDIUM RISK
HLA Restriction
ImmTAX drugs require HLA-A*02:01-positive patients, limiting the addressable population to roughly half of patients.
MEDIUM RISK
Competition in Melanoma
Cutaneous melanoma is crowded with checkpoint inhibitors and cell therapies.

Platform Breadth
