Company Overview

Janux Therapeutics (JANX) is a San Diego-based company developing TRACTr masked T cell engagers. They stay inactive in the bloodstream and switch on only in the tumor, aiming to widen the therapeutic window of T cell engagers in solid tumors.

Lead program JANX007 targets PSMA in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC); Phase 1b is enrolling taxane-naïve monotherapy and darolutamide combination cohorts. JANX014 is also in Phase 1 in mCRPC, and JANX011 is in a healthy volunteer study. The EGFR-targeted JANX008 was discontinued after Phase 1a.

Investor thesis: JANX trades close to its $970.9M cash balance, so the market assigns little value to its platform. Positive Phase 1b data in earlier-line prostate cancer could re-rate the stock.
