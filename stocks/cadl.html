Company Overview

Candel Therapeutics (CADL) is a Needham, Massachusetts-based company developing viral immunotherapies that are injected into tumors to trigger an immune response against the cancer.

Lead asset aglatimagene besadenovec (CAN-2409), an adenovirus given with an oral prodrug, significantly improved disease-free survival when added to radiotherapy in a Phase 3 trial in localized, intermediate- to high-risk prostate cancer, published in The Lancet Oncology. Candel plans to submit a BLA in Q4 2026. A Phase 3 in stage IV non-small cell lung cancer (AURORA) started in June 2026, and linoserpaturev (CAN-3110) is in Phase 1b for recurrent glioblastoma.

Investor thesis: CADL could deliver the first new immunotherapy approval in localized prostate cancer, a large market. The near-term swing factors are manufacturing validation and the BLA.
