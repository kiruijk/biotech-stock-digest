Oncolytics Biotech (ONCY)

Oncolytic Viruses

Company Overview

Oncolytics Biotech (ONCY) is a San Diego-based company developing pelareorep, an intravenously delivered reovirus that primes the immune system against tumors.

The main focus is first-line metastatic pancreatic cancer. Oncolytics has agreed with the FDA on the design of a pivotal study of pelareorep plus standard chemotherapy, supported by biomarker data from the GOBLET study. Pelareorep is also being studied in colorectal and anal cancers.

Investor thesis: ONCY is a highly speculative micro-cap. Cash was only $4.1M at June 30, 2026, against a $18.7M net loss in the first half of the year, so a financing or partnership is needed urgently. Heavy dilution is likely.
