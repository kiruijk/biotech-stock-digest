Company Overview

Replimune Group (REPL) is a Woburn, Massachusetts-based company developing oncolytic immunotherapies: modified herpes viruses that infect and kill tumor cells and trigger an immune response.

On August 6, 2026, the FDA granted accelerated approval to Tudriqev (vusolimogene oderparepvec, RP1) with nivolumab for advanced melanoma that has progressed on anti-PD-1 therapy. The approval came on the third attempt, after two complete response letters and a 10–3 favorable FDA advisory committee vote on July 30. In the IGNYTE trial, the combination showed a 33.6% response rate, 24.8-month median duration of response and 32.9-month median overall survival. Replimune expected product on the market within about 60 days of approval and raised $150M to fund the launch.

The confirmatory Phase 3 IGNYTE-3 (~400 patients) is ongoing. RP2, which adds an anti-CTLA-4 component, is in the registration-directed Phase 2/3 REVEAL trial in metastatic uveal melanoma (~280 patients).

Investor thesis: After a long regulatory fight, REPL is now a launch story. Uptake of an off-the-shelf, injectable option among community oncologists will drive the stock, alongside IGNYTE-3 (needed to keep the accelerated approval) and RP2 in uveal melanoma.
