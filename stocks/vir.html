Vir Biotechnology (VIR)

Infectious DiseaseTargeted Oncology

Company Overview

Vir Biotechnology (VIR) is a San Francisco-based company focused on chronic viral disease and oncology.

In hepatitis delta, the most severe form of viral hepatitis, Vir's combination of tobevibart (antibody) and elebsiran (siRNA) achieved undetectable virus in 88% of Phase 2 patients. The Phase 3 ECLIPSE program reads out topline data from ECLIPSE 1 in Q4 2026 and ECLIPSE 2/3 in Q1 2027. In oncology, Vir's masked T cell engagers, led by VIR-5500 (PSMA) in prostate cancer, are partnered with Astellas.

Investor thesis: Hepatitis delta has limited U.S. treatment options, making ECLIPSE a major binary event. The Astellas deal ($240M upfront plus a $75M equity investment) produced a profitable Q2 and lifted cash to ~$1.01B.
