Company Overview

Immatics (IMTX) is a Tübingen, Germany-based company developing T cell receptor (TCR)-based therapies that target cancer antigens presented inside cells, especially PRAME, which is highly expressed in melanoma and many other solid tumors.

Lead program anzu-cel (IMA203), a PRAME TCR-T cell therapy, is in the Phase 3 SUPRAME trial in melanoma. Phase 1b data showed a 56% confirmed response rate, 14.6-month median duration of response and 46% two-year survival. Topline data is expected in 1H 2027 and a BLA in 2027. Immatics also develops IMA402, an off-the-shelf PRAME bispecific, and IMA203CD8.

Investor thesis: IMTX is the leading PRAME-targeting cell therapy company, with the chance to join the small group of approved cell therapies in solid tumors. Cash of $448.2M funds operations into 2028.
