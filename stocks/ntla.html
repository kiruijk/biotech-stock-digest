Company Overview

Intellia Therapeutics (NTLA) is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based genome editing company co-founded in 2014 by Nobel laureate Jennifer Doudna. It develops in vivo CRISPR/Cas9 therapies delivered by lipid nanoparticles to the liver, aiming for one-time, potentially curative treatments.

The nearest-term asset is lonvo-z (NTLA-2002) for hereditary angioedema (HAE). In the Phase 3 HAELO trial, a single infusion reduced attacks by 87% versus placebo, met all key secondary endpoints and was published in the New England Journal of Medicine. Intellia expects FDA acceptance of the BLA in 2H 2026 and a U.S. launch in 1H 2027 — which would make it the first in vivo CRISPR therapy on the market.

The larger opportunity is nex-z (NTLA-2001) for transthyretin (ATTR) amyloidosis, partnered with Regeneron. Phase 3 MAGNITUDE (cardiomyopathy) and MAGNITUDE-2 (polyneuropathy) enrollment was paused over liver safety and has since resumed; new genomic analysis links a specific HLA allele to elevated liver enzymes, which could help manage the risk.

Investor thesis: NTLA is a bet that one-time gene editing can displace chronic therapies. A clean lonvo-z launch would validate the platform commercially, while nex-z offers much larger upside in ATTR if the safety question is resolved. The stock has fallen sharply from its highs as investors weigh the nex-z safety overhang and competition from established ATTR drugs.
