Company Overview

Wave Life Sciences (WVE) is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based RNA medicines company. Its PRISM platform designs stereopure oligonucleotides for RNA editing, RNA interference (siRNA) and exon skipping.

WVE-007 is a GalNAc-siRNA silencing INHBE, a gene linked to healthier fat distribution; the goal is fat loss while preserving muscle. The Phase 2a multidose portion of the INLIGHT obesity trial is dosing, with combination and post-incretin maintenance trials planned for 2H 2026. WVE-006, an RNA editing candidate for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (AATD) partnered with GSK, has an FDA meeting on a potential accelerated approval pathway.

Investor thesis: The stock fell ~57% in a day in March 2026 when single-dose Phase 1 INLIGHT data showed a 14% reduction in visceral fat but less than 1% placebo-adjusted weight loss at six months. The bull case now rests on multidose data showing meaningful weight loss or a clear role alongside GLP-1s, plus first-in-class RNA editing in AATD. Cash of $490.6M funds operations into 3Q 2028.
