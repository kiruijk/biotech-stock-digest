Company Overview

Prime Medicine (PRME) is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based company co-founded by David Liu to develop prime editing, a "search-and-replace" gene editing technology that can correct a wide range of mutations without double-strand DNA breaks.

Prime has refocused on liver diseases. PM577a for Wilson's disease has IND and CTA clearance, with a global Phase 1/2 study starting in 2H 2026 and data in 2027. PM647 for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (AATD) is expected to file an IND/CTA in Q3 2026.

Investor thesis: PRME offers the broadest editing technology but is still pre-clinical data in its focus areas. With $108.8M in cash and runway only into 2027, financing is a near-term overhang.
