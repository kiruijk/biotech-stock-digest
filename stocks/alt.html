Company Overview

Altimmune (ALT) is a Gaithersburg, Maryland-based clinical-stage biotech focused on liver and addiction-related diseases with a single core asset.

Pemvidutide is a GLP-1/glucagon dual receptor agonist. The glucagon component acts directly on the liver to reduce fat, which is why Altimmune is developing it for MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis). Pemvidutide received FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation based on 24-week IMPACT Phase 2b data, and the global Phase 3 PERFORMA MASH trial started in August 2026. The drug also posted positive topline data in the RECLAIM Phase 2 alcohol use disorder (AUD) trial, and the RESTORE Phase 2 alcohol-associated liver disease (ALD) trial is fully enrolled.

Investor thesis: ALT is a MASH pure play with a differentiated liver-directed mechanism and optionality in alcohol-related diseases. A $225M April 2026 raise leaves $519M in cash. The catch is time: 52-week Phase 3 MASH data is not expected until 2029.
