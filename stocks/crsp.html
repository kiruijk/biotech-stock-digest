CRISPR Therapeutics (CRSP)

Gene Editing & RNACell Therapy

Company Overview

CRISPR Therapeutics (CRSP) is a Zug, Switzerland-based gene editing company that, with partner Vertex, developed CASGEVY, the first approved CRISPR-based medicine, for sickle cell disease and beta thalassemia.

CASGEVY revenue reached $76M in Q2 2026 (+151% year over year). The FDA recently approved it for children as young as 2, adding ~5,500 eligible U.S. patients. Beyond CASGEVY, CRISPR is advancing in vivo programs such as CTX310 (ANGPTL3) for severe hypertriglyceridemia and refractory hypercholesterolemia, plus allogeneic CAR-T programs in oncology and autoimmune disease.

Investor thesis: CRSP pairs a growing commercial product with a large, well-funded pipeline. With $2.36B in cash and a $600M convertible notes deal, it has one of the strongest balance sheets in gene editing.
