Company Overview

CG Oncology (CGON) is a Dallas, Texas-based late-stage biotech focused on bladder cancer. Founded in 2010 as Cold Genesys, it is developing a single product candidate across multiple non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) settings.

Cretostimogene grenadenorepvec is an oncolytic immunotherapy delivered directly into the bladder. It is designed to replicate in and kill tumor cells while stimulating an anti-tumor immune response. Results from the pivotal BOND-003 trial in high-risk BCG-unresponsive NMIBC with carcinoma in situ were published in The Lancet Oncology in July 2026, and the company expects to complete its BLA in Q4 2026.

The second Phase 3, PIVOT-006, tests cretostimogene as adjuvant therapy in intermediate-risk NMIBC — a larger population — and has accrued the vast majority of target events with topline data expected near term. CORE-008, a multi-cohort Phase 2, extends into BCG-exposed and BCG-naïve high-risk disease.

Investor thesis: CGON is a pure-play bet on becoming a bladder-sparing standard of care in NMIBC. With ~$1B in cash and runway through 2029, it can fund the launch independently. The key questions are whether PIVOT-006 expands the label into intermediate-risk disease and how cretostimogene competes with J&J's TAR-200 and ImmunityBio's Anktiva.
